### 🎓 EduCraft

A platform designed for educational centers, combining a browser-accessible client with backend services and game-server infrastructure for classes, centers and managed learning environments.

**Core stack**

`Go` · `PostgreSQL` · `Redis` · `JWT` · `Velocity` · `Paper` · `WebAssembly` · `Stripe` · `Linux` · `Caddy`

**Current focus**

`multi-center architecture` · `classroom orchestration` · `dashboards` · `billing` · `security systems` · `backend ↔ proxy ↔ Paper integration`
